LITERATURE — verified by search, with what each ACTUALLY claims

All entries below were checked; none are from memory alone.

DRAEM  (Zavrtanik, Kristan, Skocaj — ICCV 2021)
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.07610
  Two sub-networks: a RECONSTRUCTIVE net and a DISCRIMINATIVE net. The
  discriminative net is a U-Net taking the channel-wise CONCATENATION of
  (original, reconstruction) and outputting a segmentation map. The SEGMENTATION
  is the anomaly score - it beat residual-based scoring by a large margin.
  Synthetic anomalies: Perlin noise + textures from an external dataset (DTD).
  USED FOR: C3.5U architecture, mask-based scoring, focal loss.
  CAUTION: designed for MVTec (sharp, high-contrast, textured defects).

Kascenas, Pugeault, O'Neil  (MIDL 2022) — Denoising AEs for brain MRI
  https://proceedings.mlr.press/v172/kascenas22a.html
  A coarse-noise denoising AE reaches SOTA unsupervised tumour detection, beating
  VAEs and more complex methods. KEY ARCHITECTURAL CLAIM: denoising AEs "do not
  require bottlenecks and can employ skip connections to give high resolution
  fidelity"; bottleneck architectures "tend to give poor reconstructions of not
  only the anomalous but also the normal parts".
  RELEVANCE: our CNNEncoder compresses 16384 px -> 128 dims (128:1) — exactly the
  architecture this paper argues against. That is why C3.4 (denoising objective on
  a bottleneck architecture) did NOT reproduce their gain.

IterMask^2  (Liang et al. — MICCAI 2024)
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/2406.02422
  Iterative mask refinement driven by reconstruction error + high-frequency
  structural guidance, on brain MRI. The refinement is a RULE-BASED, hand-designed
  thresholding procedure at test time.
  RELEVANCE: our K-step shared-weight LEARNED refinement operator is the learned
  counterpart. This is the clearest remaining novelty gap.

CAVGA  (Venkataramanan, Peng, Singh, Mahalanobis — ECCV 2020)
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.08616
  Convolutional Adversarial Variational AE with Guided Attention. Introduces the
  ATTENTION EXPANSION LOSS for the unsupervised setting (all training images are
  normal => nothing should be suppressed => mask should be ~1 everywhere).
  Uses a CONVOLUTIONAL latent variable to PRESERVE SPATIAL INFORMATION.
  RELEVANCE: source of our mean(1-att) expansion loss. Also the reason a
  transformer in OUR latent makes no sense (ours is a flat 128-d vector; CAVGA
  keeps a spatial latent precisely so spatial reasoning is possible).

AnatPaste  (Sato et al. — iScience 2023) — anatomy-aware CXR anomaly synthesis
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.04282
  Anatomy-restricted synthetic lesions on chest radiographs (threshold-based lung
  mask), the highest-performing UAD model in their comparison.
  RELEVANCE: source of our lung-prior restriction. IMPORTANT CAVEAT DISCOVERED
  HERE: their benefit is reported at the IMAGE level. Anatomy restriction can
  actively HARM pixel-level evaluation by inducing a geometric shortcut (see 05).

CutPaste  (Li, Sohn, Yoon, Pfister — CVPR 2021)
  Self-supervised: cut a patch, paste it elsewhere in the same image; classify.
  Background for the synthetic-anomaly family.

Focal loss  (Lin et al. — ICCV 2017, RetinaNet)
  Down-weights the easy majority class. Needed because a synthetic blob is a small
  fraction of pixels and plain BCE is cheaply satisfied by predicting "all clean".
