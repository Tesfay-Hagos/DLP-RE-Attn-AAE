--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. READING THE TABLE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 THE REPRODUCTION SUCCEEDS.
    9 of 10 methods land within one standard deviation. Four are within 0.2 points
    (AE-SSIM -0.09, AE-PL +0.08, VAE-Grad_rec +0.08, AE-Grad +0.16). Our seed spreads
    (0.35-1.41) are comparable to the published ones (0.2-1.1).

    What this validates, all at once: the dataset is the right one, the split matches,
    the [-1,1] normalisation is correct, the architectures are faithful (parameter counts
    agree: AE 2,351,184 vs the published 2.35M), and the training loop is sound.

2.2 THE ONE MISS IS DAE, AND IT IS NOT RESOLVABLE AT n=1.
    83.87 vs 86.1 +- 0.7. Being a single seed, a real implementation gap cannot be
    separated from seed variation. Two more seeds would cost ~2.7 h. Until then this
    row must be reported as n=1 with the gap stated openly, not quietly rounded away.

2.3 THE ORDERING IS THE INTERESTING PART.

      87.58  AE-PL          <- best, but requires ImageNet weights
      86.86  AE-U           <- within 0.7 of it, and needs NO pre-training
      83.87  DAE (n=1)      <- also no pre-training, 13x the parameters
      80.81  AE-SSIM
      73.86  AE-Grad
      68.53  AE-l1          }
      68.35  AE             }  statistically indistinguishable from one another
      67.84  VAE-Grad_combi }
      67.33  VAE            }

    Three groupings worth naming in the report:

    (a) THE FLAT CLUSTER (67-69). AE, AE-l1, VAE and VAE-Grad_combi differ by less than
        our seed noise. Changing the pixel norm from L2 to L1 does nothing. Making the
        latent space probabilistic does nothing. On this dataset, the standard levers on
        the RECONSTRUCTION side simply do not move the result.

    (b) THE METRIC JUMP (+12 to +19). Everything that beats the cluster changed HOW THE
        RESIDUAL IS MEASURED — a structural metric, a deep feature distance, an input
        gradient, or an uncertainty weighting. None of them changed the bottleneck.

    (c) THE PRE-TRAINING QUESTION. The best method uses ImageNet features. The second
        best (AE-U) does not, and costs 256 extra parameters. That is the observation
        that motivates experiments A3 and A4.

2.4 WHY THE PLAIN AE IS SO WEAK — the mechanism the rest of the project builds on.
    Squared pixel error on a chest radiograph is dominated by high-contrast ANATOMY:
    rib edges, diaphragm, mediastinal borders. Those structures produce large residuals
    in every image, normal or not. Pneumonia is a diffuse, low-contrast opacity that
    barely registers on that scale. The score is therefore mostly measuring "how hard was
    this image to reconstruct", which is nearly independent of whether it is abnormal.
    68.35 is what that looks like.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. WHAT THIS STEP DOES *NOT* SHOW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    It does not explain WHY AE-SSIM beats AE, or WHY AE-U beats both. Each of those
    methods changed the objective and the score together, so the table alone cannot
    attribute the gain to either. That is precisely the gap experiment A1 addresses.
