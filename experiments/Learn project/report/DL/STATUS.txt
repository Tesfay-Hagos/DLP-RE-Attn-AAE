RESOLVED — DO NOT ADD BraTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The DL scientist WITHDREW its earlier "necessary rather than nice to have", which it
says was conditioned on keeping the modality claim; retracting the claim was the
better and cheaper resolution. Its decisive argument: NO BraTS OUTCOME CAN FALSIFY
THE MODALITY ACCOUNT -- positive reads as "helps on CXR and MRI, hurts on fundus",
negative as "helps on CXR only". Only a WITHIN-modality dissociation could break the
confound. BraTS also maximises two of the paper's own mechanisms (skull-stripped MRI
is mostly exact-zero background, where the range-drift restoring force is weakest and
SSIM is ~1 on flat regions), so it would add a fourth uncontrolled factor.

Reviewer severity if we omit it: "the authors acknowledge this", NOT reject-level --
but ONLY if no surviving sentence contradicts Limitation 2. Items 1, 2 and 7 above
currently do, which would escalate it to a soundness concern.
