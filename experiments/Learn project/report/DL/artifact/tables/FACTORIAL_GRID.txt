------------------------------------------------------------------------------
READING THE GRID
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * The DIAGONAL is where the literature lives: every published method in this family
    sits on it, because every one sets d_train = d_score.
  * The l2 COLUMN below the first row is the cost of mismatching -- a model trained
    under one metric, read with plain pixel error. On RSNA the perceptual row falls to
    44.88, BELOW CHANCE.
  * The aeu COLUMN is empty off its own row: the uncertainty score needs a per-pixel
    variance that only that objective emits. The frozen-backbone head supplies those
    three cells by another route.
  * The SSIM column, first row, is the substitution Lagogiannis et al. recommend:
    RSNA 77.42 (+9.08 over 68.35), VinCXR 53.65 (-2.37), LAG 68.43 (-10.54).
