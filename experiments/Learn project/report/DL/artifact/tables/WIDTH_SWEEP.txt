WHAT THIS SHOWS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. THE PLATEAU IS REAL BUT NOT UNIFORM. Widths 2, 4 and 8 agree to within 0.23
     points on RSNA, 0.78 on LAG and 1.77 on VinCXR. The direction and significance
     of every cross-dataset comparison that uses the head is unchanged across all
     three widths; the magnitudes move by at most 1.77 points.

  2. THE SELECTED WIDTH IS NOT THE BEST WIDTH ON EITHER HELD-OUT DATASET.
     RSNA selected w=2. The best width is w=4 on VinCXR (70.18 vs 68.41 at w=2)
     and w=4 on LAG (83.56 vs 83.27 at w=2). Carrying the RSNA choice
     unchanged therefore UNDERSTATES the head on both held-out datasets. The
     selection did not flatter the transfer result; it cost it.

  3. THE TWO WIDTHS THAT DIFFER ARE INFORMATIVE, NOT COMPETITIVE.
     w=1  is a LINEAR head: BasicBlock(..., last_layer=True) strips BatchNorm and
          ReLU, leaving a single ConvTranspose2d. It fails on all three datasets.
     w=16 is an optimisation failure, not a capacity result: initial training losses
          of 619 / 3,381 / 54,824 across seeds, +-4.77 spread on RSNA.

  4. A LABEL-FREE RULE EXISTS AND CHANGES NOTHING MATERIAL. Final training loss on
     training normals never sees a test image or a label. It selects w=8 on RSNA
     (-4.1594 vs -4.1267 at w=2), because deeper heads fit the training objective
     better. The RSNA head then scores 81.81 rather than 82.00. No reported
     conclusion changes.
