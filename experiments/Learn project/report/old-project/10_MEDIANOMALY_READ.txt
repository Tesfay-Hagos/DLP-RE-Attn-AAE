################ A. THINGS THAT INVALIDATE DIRECT COMPARISON ################

A1. THEIR RSNA SPLIT != OURS.  (Sec 3.1.1, Table 2)
    Theirs : 3851 normal TRAIN ; TEST = 1000 normal + 1000 abnormal
    Ours   : TEST_NORMAL=2000 + TEST_OPACITY=2000
    => our AUCs are NOT directly comparable to their 87.5 / 86.5 / 80.0.
    ACTION: switch to 1000+1000 test if we want to quote their table as baselines.

A2. THEY EXPLICITLY REJECT PIXEL-LEVEL AUC.  (Sec 3.1.4)
    "We do not employ the pixel-level AUC since it is insensitive to false
     positives, which is particularly relevant in medical images where the
     majority of pixels are negative."
    They use AP_pix and best-possible Dice ([Dice]) instead.
    => WE HAVE BEEN USING PIXEL-AUROC THROUGHOUT. Add AP_pix + Dice.
    => *** THIS IS ALSO OUR OPENING ***: they ASSERT pixel-AUC is bad; we
       MEASURED how bad, with a fixed-anatomy control (lung prior = 0.7540
       pooled, 0.5000 in-lung). That is concrete evidence for their assertion,
       plus a constructive fix. Cite them, then quantify.

A3. INPUT SIZE / NETWORK SIZE (Sec 4.2.1, Tables 8-11)
    "using an input size of 64x64 achieves comparable performance to ... 128x128"
    "increasing the depth or width of the reconstruction networks does not yield
     performance gains"
    CONCLUSION (their words): "reconstruction-based AD on medical images does NOT
    benefit from large resolutions or large networks."
    => We used 128x128. 64x64 would be ~4x cheaper at equal performance.

################ B. WHERE OUR RESULTS CORROBORATE / EXTEND THEM ################

B1. OUR CAPACITY FINDING IS INDEPENDENT CONFIRMATION + AN EXTENSION.
    They: bigger networks do not help reconstruction-based medical AD.
    We  : bigger networks actively HURT under synthetic supervision, and we
          measured the mechanism -- 2,641-param head -> 0.675 in-lung (real
          signal); 1,928,129-param U-Net -> 1.0000 synthetic / 0.489 real (chance).
    => They observe "no gain"; we show "active harm, via proxy memorisation",
       with the synthetic-vs-real gap as the measurement.

B2. THEY STATE THE SSL FAILURE MECHANISM; WE QUANTIFIED IT.  (Sec 4.1)
    "reconstruction methods significantly outperform SSL methods in pixel-level
     AnoSeg ... SSL methods trained through synthetic anomaly classification rely
     on the similarity between synthetic abnormal patterns and real anomalies.
     They struggle to recognize real abnormal regions when these regions exhibit
     significant differences in appearance compared to synthetic anomalies."
    Ours: synthetic 1.0000 vs real 0.6110 image AUC; in-lung 0.4885 (chance),
          reproduced across TWO generators differing 5x in lesion size
          (coverage 0.247 -> 0.051 changed real performance by 0.0002).
    => We provide the missing quantification of a mechanism they only describe.

B3. THEY CALL ANATPASTE UNSTABLE — consistent with our negative result. (Sec 4.1)
    "excluding the use of ImageNet weights, the SOTA SSL method is the two-stage
     AnatPaste. However, this method exhibits unstable performance across
     different datasets. It fails to outperform the vanilla AE on LAG and
     BraTS2021, indicating a high sensitivity to abnormal patterns."
    => Our anatomy-restricted generator underperforming is not an implementation
       failure; the benchmark reports the same fragility.

B4. PIXEL-LEVEL ON RSNA IS A GAP. (Sec 3.1.2, Table 2)
    Their AnoSeg is evaluated on BraTS2021 ONLY (voxel-level tumour masks).
    RSNA is AnoCls only. => Pixel-level AD on chest radiographs with box-derived
    GT is NOT covered by the benchmark. That is our space.

################ C. THE BEST METHODS TO ADOPT ################

C1. IMAGE-LEVEL (AnoCls), their SOTA: AE-PL, AE-U, FAE-SSIM.
    RSNA: AE-PL 87.5 | AE-U 86.5 | GANomaly 80.0.
    "the ImageNet weights are highly effective and powerful for medical AD" —
    most SOTA relies on them; AE-U and DAE are the exceptions that do NOT.

C2. PIXEL-LEVEL (AnoSeg), their SOTA: **DAE (denoising AE with a customised UNet)**
    "the best AnoSeg is achieved by DAE, surpassing the second-best method by a
     margin of nearly 20% [Dice] ... attributed to the customized UNet
     architecture ... The denoising training approach utilized by DAE effectively
     overcomes [the skip-connection leakage] issue."
    NOTE the tension they resolve: Mao et al. 2020 showed UNet skip connections
    LEAK abnormal information to the decoder; denoising training fixes that.
    => OUR C3.4 (denoising AE) and C3.5U (UNet) are each half of DAE. Combining
       them correctly = denoising-trained UNet = their best segmentation method.
       This is the strongest positive method available to us for LOCALISATION.
