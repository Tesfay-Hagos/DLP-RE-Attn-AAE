================================================================================
NEGATIVE RESULTS — what did NOT work, and the evidence
================================================================================
These are findings, not failures. Each is backed by a controlled experiment.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
N1. Adversarial latent regularisation (AAE) DEGRADES detection
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  C3 < C1 at EVERY lambda over a 20x range (0.05 - 1.0), 2 seeds each.
  Gap 0.004 - 0.011; seed variance far below it.
  Consistent with Baur et al. (2021).
  CAVEAT: the sweep was flat because of Adam scale-invariance (see 01), so it
  established "adversarial on vs off", not a strength curve.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
N2. The latent discriminator score carries NO anomaly information
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  disc AUC-ROC across the lambda sweep: 0.4986, 0.5229, 0.4652, 0.5067, 0.3876.
  Chance, or inverted with huge variance (std 0.086 at lambda=1.0).
  Independently, old C4's disc score = exactly 0.5000.
  CONSEQUENCES:
    - fusion (0.5*SSIM + 0.5*disc) always LOSES to SSIM alone -> never report it
    - the "latent quality" comparison column has no content
    - CRITICAL: this dead signal was the ONLY thing training re_attn in old C4,
      which is why its mask collapsed to a constant
  INTERPRETATION: prior matching worked TOO well - anomalies are absorbed
  indistinguishably into the Gaussian ball.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
N3. Attention mask collapse (old C4, closed-loop version)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  att_mean 0.548 -> 0.994 (ep10) -> 1.000 (ep30+), att_std -> 0.0001.
  Collapsed to a CONSTANT no-op mask.
  Root cause: training data is 100% normal, so nothing ever rewards CLOSING the
  mask; the expansion loss mean(1-att) rewards opening it, unopposed.
  We had guarded the wrong corner (we feared att->0 blanking the input; it went
  att->1 instead).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
N4. Iteration helps DETECTION but HURTS LOCALIZATION (C3.4b, fresh inference)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  K:        1        2        3        4
  image:  0.9309   0.9464   0.9500   0.9502    (monotonic up, saturating)
  in-lung:0.6783   0.6558   0.6539   0.6525    (monotonic down)
  Clean trade-off; each refinement step closes the mask harder overall (better
  global score) but less precisely (worse overlap). [numbers CONTESTED, see 02]

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
N5. A bigger, "correct" architecture made real performance WORSE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  C3.5U (U-Net, no bottleneck, 68px receptive field) vs C3.4b (128:1 bottleneck,
  5px receptive field head):
    synthetic task:  C3.5U essentially perfect (1.0000)
    real detection:  C3.5U 0.6110  vs  C1 0.8166  -> WORSE than a plain AE
    real localization: 0.4941 in-lung -> CHANCE
  INTERPRETATION (important): MORE CAPACITY -> BETTER PROXY FIT -> WORSE TRANSFER.
  The 2,641-parameter head in C3.4b was too weak to memorise the generator's
  signature, so it stayed generic and partly transferred. The 1.9M U-Net learned
  the exact signature ("+0.35 uniform brightness, smooth blob, soft edges, inside
  these two ellipses") which real consolidation does not have.
  => The architectural diagnosis was correct, but fixing the architecture EXPOSED
     a deeper problem it had been masking.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
N6. Denoising training alone is NEUTRAL here (unlike brain MRI)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  C3.4-a 0.8467 vs C1 0.8166. Kascenas et al. found denoising AEs reach SOTA on
  brain MRI; here the effect is small. Plausible domain reason: pneumonic
  consolidation is diffuse and low-contrast, unlike focal bright MRI lesions.
  (n=1 for the valid config - needs 2 more seeds before stating this firmly.)
