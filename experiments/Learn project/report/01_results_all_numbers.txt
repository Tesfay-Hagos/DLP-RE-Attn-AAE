--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
LAMBDA_ADV SWEEP for C3 (5 values x 2 seeds), SSIM AUC-ROC mean
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  0.05 -> 0.8082 | 0.10 -> 0.8068 | 0.30 -> 0.8058 | 0.60 -> 0.8074 | 1.00 -> 0.8125
  ALL below C1 = 0.8166. Discriminator score at each: 0.4986 / 0.5229 / 0.4652 /
  0.5067 / 0.3876  -> chance or inverted, everywhere.

  CAVEAT (important, admit this in the write-up): the sweep is nearly FLAT because
  opt_gen_c3 is a SEPARATE Adam optimiser. Adam is scale-invariant (m_hat and
  sqrt(v_hat) both scale with lambda and cancel), and clip_grad_norm_(1.0) caps the
  rest. So the sweep tested "adversarial step on vs off", NOT strength. To sweep
  lambda properly, fold terms into ONE backward pass on ONE optimiser.
  ALSO: BEST_LAMBDA_ADV was selected on the TEST set. Note as a limitation.

REVISED CONCLUSIONS:
  - ITERATION PURELY HURTS. K=1 dominates on image AUC and in-lung. The earlier
    "detection improves / localization degrades trade-off" was an artifact of the
    BatchNorm bug. Report K=1.
  - C3.4b K=1 (0.8490) vs C3.4-a denoising control (0.8467) = +0.002 -> WITHIN NOISE.
    The attention adds nothing at the IMAGE level over the denoising control.
  - BUT in-lung 0.6748 vs chance 0.5000 is a REAL localisation signal (+0.175).
    C3.4b is the best localizer produced in this project.
